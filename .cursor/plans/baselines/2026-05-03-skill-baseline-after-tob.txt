During Plan B execution, the IDE working tree silently reverted 5 modified files
(the 4 skills + memory/07-decisions-log.md) AFTER each commit completed
successfully. Diagnosis: Cursor IDE buffer cache held the pre-edit state and
"saved" it back over the on-disk version when the file lost focus or auto-save
fired. Symptom: evaluator scored prototype-designer 75/100 even though the fix
commit (8b1f912) was on the branch.

Fix during this session: `git restore <files>` to overwrite working tree from
HEAD before re-running the evaluator.

Lesson candidate (for cross-project promotion): when the IDE has files open
during programmatic edits via the agent, ALWAYS verify with `git status` and
`git diff HEAD` after the commit, not just after the edit. The commit may be
correct while the working tree is silently stale.
